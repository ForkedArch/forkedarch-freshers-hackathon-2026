# ForkedArch Freshers Hackathon 2026 — Official Rulebook

**Organizer:** ForkedArch  
**Participants:** KUET CSE 2K25 — 1-1 Students  
**Format:** Fully Online  
**Duration:** 50 Hours

## 1. Eligibility

- Only KUET CSE 2K25 — 1-1 students may participate.
- Each participant may belong to one team only.
- Each team must contain exactly 4 members.
- Participant information must be accurate.

## 2. Female Team Member Score Boost

Teams with at least one female team member receive a **5% multiplier on their final judging score**.

Example: `80 × 1.05 = 84`.

False representation may result in disqualification.

## 3. Format and Duration

After the official challenge is released:

- 48 consecutive hours are provided for development.
- An additional 2 hours are provided for final submission.
- Total duration: **50 hours**.

Exact timestamps will be announced by ForkedArch.

## 4. Challenge

Each registered team will receive its problem statement, challenge details, requirements, and submission instructions by email after registration and verification.

## 5. Technology

Any appropriate programming language, framework, library, database, API, cloud platform, design tool, or development environment may be used.

## 6. AI Tools

AI tools are allowed. Participants remain responsible for all submitted work and must understand it.

Significant AI usage should be disclosed in the README, including tools used and what they contributed. Judges may ask teams to explain AI-assisted components.

## 7. Quality Assurance

Judges may consider functional correctness, stability, validation, error handling, security, usability, testing, reliability, documentation, and overall polish.

## 8. GitHub Requirement

Every project must be pushed to GitHub. The repository should contain relevant source code and documentation. Teams are encouraged to use Git throughout development and maintain meaningful commits.

## 9. README Requirement

A comprehensive `README.md` is mandatory. It must explain, as applicable:

- Project overview
- Problem
- Solution
- Features
- Technology stack
- Architecture
- Complete workflow
- Setup
- Environment variables
- API documentation
- Database structure
- AI usage
- Testing/QA
- Limitations
- Future improvements

## 10. Presentation Video

Every team must submit a presentation video of **less than 3 minutes** covering the problem, solution, key features, technology, workflow, and demonstration.

## 11. Submission

Required items:

- Team information
- GitHub repository
- README.md
- Presentation video
- Demo link, where applicable
- Any additional information requested by ForkedArch

## 12. Late Submission

Late submissions are generally not accepted. Teams should submit early enough to account for technical issues.

If an official platform experiences a significant outage, ForkedArch may announce an alternative process or deadline.

## 13. Open-Source Resources

Open-source libraries, public documentation, tutorials, APIs, and legitimate online resources are allowed. Teams must respect applicable licenses and credit significant third-party resources where appropriate.

## 14. Pre-existing Work

The core solution must be developed during the hackathon. General boilerplate and standard starter configuration may be used. Significant pre-existing components should be disclosed.

## 15. Security

Do not commit API keys, passwords, database credentials, private tokens, JWT secrets, cloud credentials, or other secrets.

Participants must not attack, scan, exploit, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to other teams, ForkedArch, KUET, or third-party systems.

## 16. Integrity

The following may result in disqualification:

- Plagiarism
- Submitting another team's work
- Purchasing or obtaining a pre-built solution and presenting it as original
- Submitting a substantially pre-built project as the hackathon solution
- Falsifying team information
- Misleading judges
- Unauthorized access or malicious activity
- Serious conduct violations
- Failure to satisfy mandatory submission requirements

## 17. Judging

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Problem Understanding & Impact | 20% |
| Innovation & Creativity | 20% |
| Technical Implementation | 25% |
| UI/UX & Usability | 15% |
| Completeness & QA | 10% |
| Presentation & Communication | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

The applicable 5% female-member score boost is applied after standard judging.

## 18. Technical Verification

Judges may request a live demonstration, screen share, code explanation, architecture explanation, or explanation of AI-generated components.

## 19. Conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully. Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and malicious interference are prohibited.

See [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## 20. Intellectual Property

Teams retain ownership of their original project, subject to third-party licenses.

By participating, teams grant ForkedArch permission to publicly display team names, project names, descriptions, repository links, demo/video links, and event-related media for educational and promotional purposes.

## 21. Tie-Breaking

If scores are tied:

1. Technical Implementation
2. Problem Understanding & Impact
3. Innovation & Creativity
4. Judges' consensus

## 22. Organizer Authority

ForkedArch may clarify rules, address unforeseen issues, resolve disputes, investigate violations, and make final decisions regarding eligibility, judging, and disqualification.

**Build. Explain. Ship.**
