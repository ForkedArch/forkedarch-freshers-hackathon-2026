# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ForkedArch is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in the Freshers Hackathon.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat others with respect.
- Communicate professionally.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Give appropriate credit.
- Use technology responsibly.
- Follow the hackathon rules.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or threats
- Discriminatory or abusive behavior
- Personal attacks
- Intimidation
- Deliberate disruption of another team's work
- Unauthorized access to accounts or systems
- Malicious attacks
- Fraudulent representation
- Plagiarism
- Sharing another participant's private information without permission

## Reporting

Report serious misconduct to the ForkedArch organizing team through the official communication channel as soon as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

ForkedArch may investigate reports and take appropriate action, including warnings, removal from event channels, score penalties, or disqualification.

## Scope

This code applies to hackathon-related communication, repositories, events, meetings, and other spaces directly associated with ForkedArch Freshers Hackathon 2026.
